# Open questions

Last updated: 2026-08-21

## Structural

1. **Does the report still have a thesis?** The original premise — a shadow liquidity
   multiplier that blunts or bypasses QT — is not supported by the velocity or FX swap
   turnover evidence. Both are flat and balance-sheet constrained. Decide whether the
   paper becomes: (a) *why the collateral channel did NOT amplify*, and where the
   expansion went instead; or (b) a narrower piece on Pillar C circularity only.
   Option (a) is the more original argument and is well evidenced.

2. **Is the collateral channel and the FX swap channel one phenomenon or two?**
   Argument for one: both are collateralised short-term funding, both booked off or
   netted off balance sheet, both plateaued for the same Basel III reason. Argument
   for two: FX swaps transform currency denomination rather than multiplying claims
   against the same security. Resolving this determines the report's spine.

3. **Where did the expansion actually go?** Working answer: entity-level balance sheet
   growth in the NBFI/insurer layer, not velocity. Needs a series. FSB narrow measure
   is the closest but is annual and lags.

## Quantitative gaps

4. **No sourceable Pillar C aggregate.** FABNs, NAV loans, CFOs, SRT wraps. Options:
   leave unquantified and say so; or build a bottom-up sample from the largest
   PE-owned insurers' statutory filings and state the coverage ratio explicitly.
   Do not extrapolate to a system total.

5. **Hyperscaler off-balance-sheet "shadow borrowing."** BIS Bulletin No. 120
   (Eren, Krohn & Todorov) is a first attempt, not a maintained series. Check whether
   anything has followed it.

6. **The dynamic haircut model needs empirical anchoring.** `C = V₀(1 − h(t))` with
   non-linear silicon obsolescence (Hopper → Blackwell → Rubin) is currently
   assumption, not data. Need observed GPU residual values or lender advance rates.
   The CoreWeave DDTL repricing is one observable; find more.

7. **Is the equity flow multiplier stable in a concentrated market?** Gabaix–Koijen's
   ~5x is an aggregate estimate. Whether it is higher for the mega-cap AI cohort
   specifically — smaller effective float, more mechanical passive demand — is the
   interesting question and may be testable.
