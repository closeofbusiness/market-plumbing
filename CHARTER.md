# Charter

## The mission

> Establish, in numbers with ranges rather than false precision, what is driving asset prices and where the money is coming from: above all the run-up in US equities and the funding of the AI build-out and the IPO boom, and also rates, FX and commodities. Test every channel against fundamentals (earnings, interest rates and risk premia) as the benchmark — direct money creation, shadow banking and shadow money, the collateral channel, market structure such as the passive bid and ETF creation, and any other driver the evidence reveals — and record a channel that turns out not to matter as a result. For every flow, name the buyer, the seller and the funding; treat every claim as a hypothesis until it is tested; use free data only; and publish the work openly.

This is the programme's standing goal: the principal asked for it on 25 September 2026 to replace scattered statements of the goal ([THE_ASK.md](THE_ASK.md), E-015). It is built from the principal's own words in E-003, E-004, E-005 and E-007, and the principal confirmed this wording on 26 Sep (E-016). The sections below are how it is carried out; none of them is a second goal.

It widened an earlier goal: a fundamental understanding of shadow-banking collateral and money creation on the Pozsar–Singh nonbank–bank nexus. The nexus work carries over as two of the channels. Michael Green's market-structure arguments (the passive bid, ETF creation) are hypotheses to size, not conclusions. For commodities, the fundamentals benchmark is the physical balance and the inventory path.

## Three questions, in this order

Every update runs in this order. The live answer uses the same order.

1. **Prices.** Decompose the move.
   - Equities: earnings versus the multiple, and whether valuation is cheaper money or a premium holding up against real yields and funding costs.
   - Rates: carry, roll, the expected path of policy and inflation, versus term premium.
   - Commodities: physical balance, inventory, curve shape, versus risk premium.
2. **Money, as measurement.** Who bought, against what net supply, after wrappers are classified — equities, duration, and commodity paper. Use the same denominators across sectors and across cross-border legs. A dollar, yen, or euro of hedged demand is not counted twice.
3. **Channels.** Size every path, including the ones that fail:
   - direct money creation
   - wholesale repo and shadow money
   - collateral re-use
   - passive and ETF structure
   - official-sector plumbing, including reverse-repo handoffs into dealer funding
   - cross-border and FX-funded sovereign demand
   - commodity inventory and curve financing
   - cross-checks against long-money holders

   A path the evidence opens is added. A path that fails stays in the record.

Rates and commodities are first-class. They are updated on this cadence. They are not backdrop for an equity note.

## Scope

- Equities, with the recent US window already studied.
- Core rates: US Treasuries, JGBs, euro-area government bonds.
- FX and cross-currency flows: basis, swaps, reserve recycling, private carry, official and private cross-border demand.
- Commodities: energy, metals, softs — inventory, term structure, producer hedging, and futures, ETPs, and swaps versus physical supply.

Named stress cases use the same three questions:

- Equity: AI funding and the IPO boom.
- Rates and FX: fiscal supply, QT/QE, cross-currency basis regimes.
- Commodities: inventory shocks, OPEC+ and metals supply, commodity index or ETP demand waves.

## Rules

These bind the charter and every product.

- **Free data only.** No paid series. A missing paid feed is not a reason to invent a number, and it is not, by itself, a reason to call a design blocked when the limit is arithmetic.
- **Rough bands over false precision.** A direction with a wide band is a result. A point figure with no denominator is not.
- **Every claim stays a hypothesis until tested.** Survival of a review raises credence. It does not settle the claim. An identity that sums exactly cannot fail, so the sum is not evidence about the world.
- **Attributions must add up.** A dollar is counted once. The residual is stated.
- **Every purchase has a seller.** Name the net buyer, the funding, and the net supply. Money does not “flow into” a market in aggregate.
- **Negatives stay first-class.** A channel that did not carry the move is a result.
- **Nothing is settled merely because it survived last month.** Re-test when a new print lands.
- **Do not claim that FX, official buying, or financial commodity flows caused a yield or curve move.** Presence of a flow is a measurement. Causation is a different object.
- **Do not turn “money was present” into a causal aggregate equity-price impact.** A prior design that tried to do that closed on free data. Carry that limit until a better design exists. See [corrections/REGISTER.md](corrections/REGISTER.md).
- **Do not republish verbatim copyrighted or paywalled third-party documents.** Summarise and cite. The programme’s own write-ups may be committed.

## Grades

Used the same way in the live answer and the dossiers.

| Grade | Means | Does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| IDENTITY | Holds by construction, given its inputs | Anything about the world |
| MEASURED | Observed on a stated universe and denominator | That the universe or the tags were the right ones |
| BOUNDED | A ceiling or a floor, one side only | A point estimate |
| HYPOTHESIS | Untested, or tested and still standing | Established |

## Where the equity work stands relative to the rest

The equity price decomposition, the equity-supply identity after stripping ETF wrappers, and the channel tests for shadow money, collateral, AI funding, and IPOs are written up. A full rates decomposition (carry, roll, expected path, term premium) for US Treasuries, JGBs, and euro-area government bonds is not. A commodity physical-balance and curve decomposition is not. Those sections stay open on purpose.
