# EPS SPLIT — aggregate profit vs per-share accretion in S&P 500 EPS growth (18 Sep 2026)

**Status: DATA.** Ranked item 8. Spec `_research/2026-09-18-EPS-Split-Spec.md`.
**Panel:** `data/eps_split/` (companyfacts diluted WASO, Yahoo split-adjusted; 2015 YE mcap weights).
**Identity (accounting, not causal):** Δlog EPS = Δlog aggregate earnings − Δlog diluted shares.
**Bans honored:** no scalar M; no causal “buybacks drove prices”; EFA NFC aggregates not used for the headline split.

---

> **[C-093]** **Method verified; headline overstated.** AAPL re-derived exactly from SEC (23,172,276,000 → 15,004,697,000 split-adjusted diluted shares, Δlog −0.4346). But the **13.4% below is the top of the doc's own 7.1–13.4% weighting band**, survivorship pushes all four up, and **"aggregate profit" is a residual that absorbs index composition** by construction. Carry **accretion ~7–13%** and **profit + composition ~69–75%**. The lead claim survives: earnings did most of the work, and it is mostly real profit.

## Headline (answer terms)

Of the S&P 500’s cumulative **log price** gain since 2015 (Shiller P ×3.63, Δlog P = 1.289):

| Component | Share of Δlog price | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| **Aggregate profit growth** | **68.9%** | Δlog AggE = 0.889 |
| **Per-share accretion** (share-count decline) | **13.4%** | −Δlog shares = 0.173 |
| **Multiple expansion** (P2a residual) | **17.7%** | Δlog P/E = 0.228 |
| **Sum** | **100%** | exact by construction |

**One-line answer:** ~**69%** of the price gain is aggregate profit growth; ~**13%** is per-share accretion; ~**18%** is the multiple (P2a).

Of the **EPS** factor itself (Shiller E ×2.89, Δlog EPS = 1.061 = P2a’s ~82% of price):

| Split of Δlog EPS | Share |
|---|---:|
| Aggregate profit | **83.7%** |
| Per-share accretion | **16.3%** |

So the lead claim “earnings did 70–80% of the work” is mostly **real profit**. Accretion is material but secondary (~1/6 of EPS growth; ~1/8 of the price gain under 2015 weights).

**Weighting band for accretion’s share of price** (same panel, alternate aggregators): equal-weight 7.1%; latest-mcap 8.3%; Laspeyres share-index 10.6%; **headline 2015-mcap mean of firm Δlogs 13.4%**.

## Method

1. **Universe:** current S&P 500 constituents from Wikipedia (`List of S&P 500 companies`, fetched 18 Sep 2026) → CIK map via `https://www.sec.gov/files/company_tickers.json` (503 tickers mapped).
2. **Share counts:** SEC companyfacts per CIK (`WeightedAverageNumberOfDilutedSharesOutstanding`, FY 10-K). FY facts retained only when period-end calendar year ∈ {fy, fy+1}.
3. **Split adjustment:** Yahoo Finance split history; multiply raw WASO by product of splits **strictly after** fiscal period end through 2026-09-18 share basis.
4. **Weights:** 2015 year-end price × split-adjusted diluted shares → Laspeyres mcap weights. Headline Δlog shares = Σ wᵢ Δlog(sharesᵢ).
5. **Aggregate earnings:** inferred by identity: Δlog AggE = Δlog EPS_Shiller + Δlog shares (Shiller factors from P2a: E ×2.89, P ×3.63).
6. **Not used for headline:** Fed EFA NFC issuance/repurchase aggregates (public buybacks netted against private issuance — does not answer S&P EPS accretion).

User-Agent on sec.gov / data.sec.gov only: `ThirdDerivativeResearch/1.0`. Foreground pulls.

## Threats named

- **Survivorship:** current constituents over 2015–2025 overstate buyback intensity (survivors are the buyers). Bias direction: accretion likely **overstated** vs a back-dated universe.
- **Index composition:** ~~not folded into either bucket here~~ **CORRECTED 19 Sep (C-093): it IS folded in — into "aggregate profit".** Aggregate earnings is inferred as Shiller index EPS (which moves as constituents change) plus the current-constituent share change, so composition lands in the profit residual by construction. Direction unmeasured.
- **Coverage:** 385/500; missing megas GOOGL/GOOG/BRK-B/TSLA/AVGO — a panel light on Alphabet/Berkshire/Tesla/Broadcom is not a full test of those names.
