THE QUESTION
In the US Financial Accounts (the Federal Reserve's Z.1), the "Households and nonprofit organizations"
sector is computed as a RESIDUAL: total corporate equity minus every separately measured holding sector.
It is consequently the largest and least identified holder of US equities.

**Who has tried to decompose it, and how?** I want to know whether a body of work exists on measuring who
actually holds and buys US equities beyond that residual, and what methods it uses.
