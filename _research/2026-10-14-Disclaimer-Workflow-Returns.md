# Disclaimer workflow, 14 Oct 2026: reviews and editorial decisions

*At the principal's request, a seven-agent workflow (Sonnet) drafted the disclaimer from three lenses (plain English, securities-regulatory, liability and IP), merged the drafts, and put the merge through two adversarial reviews (coverage and false claims; tone and consistency) before a final edit. The supervisor then made two wording changes and, because the coverage review found the README and page claiming third-party files are not redistributed while about a hundred are committed (data/THIRD_PARTY_MANIFEST.tsv), corrected those statements. The published text is [DISCLAIMER.md](../DISCLAIMER.md).*

## Editor's decisions (final pass)

- Owner point 1: the views are the author's own personal opinion, published in a personal capacity, nothing to do with the author's employer, and not the views of any employer (past or present) or other organisation. 'Author' is defined once as the person who directs the project; 'principal' is not used. Review fix applied: 'nothing to do with' is kept for the employer only, and 'does not represent' is used for everyone else.
- Owner point 2: not investment, financial, legal, accounting or tax advice; not an offer, solicitation or recommendation to buy, sell or hold any asset; no adviser-client, fiduciary or other professional relationship.
- Owner point 3: published purely for academic interest and entertainment, worded as 'not a suitable basis for decisions about money' (a statement about the work, not a use restriction that would sit badly with CC BY 4.0).
- Owner point 4 (standard items): take your own investment, legal, accounting and tax advice from suitably qualified professionals; decisions and risk are the reader's; investing risks loss of capital; past performance does not predict results; as-is, no warranty; no liability to the fullest extent the law allows, saving liability that cannot lawfully be excluded; views may change without notice, no duty to update. No statutory citations.
- BLOCKING FIX applied: the false 'third-party material is not redistributed' sentence is replaced. Third-party material keeps its own terms outside CC BY 4.0; some third-party files are included so results can be rebuilt; data/THIRD_PARTY_MANIFEST.tsv lists them. I re-checked: the manifest exists, is tracked, and 100 of its listed files are tracked in git.
- Review fix applied: forward-looking sentence now names the pre-registered predictions in the repository (Analysis/Forward_Prediction_Register.md, titled PRE-REGISTERED FORWARD PREDICTION REGISTER, exists) and calls them speculative, so the file cannot be quoted against the disclaimer.
- Review fix applied: opinion versus AI authorship reconciled by 'even where AI models drafted them'; AI wording follows the README ('AI models do the research and the writing, directed by the author'), so the three texts agree.
- Review fix applied: liability now covers the author, anyone publishing under the closeofbusiness byline, and any contributor; endorsement language tracks the licensor (closeofbusiness) as well as the author; scripts and data are included in the as-is clause.
- Review fix applied: statements about named companies, people or publications come from cited sources via AI models, may be wrong, and a summary of what someone said is not a statement by them; errors and rights-holder removal requests go through repository issues (issues are enabled).
- Review fix applied: dates, vintages and copies; the overview page and CORRECTIONS.md give the current position; older notes, versions, forks and copies may hold withdrawn claims; free public data can be revised, delayed or wrong.
- Review fix applied: 'Authoritative' is explained as which document wins, not a claim of correctness.
- Review fix applied: CORRECTIONS.md is described as 'more than a hundred corrections, among them wrong figures and a cited paper that does not exist'. I verified there are 145 entries and that C-079 is titled 'a paper that does not exist', so the claim is accurate and cannot go stale. The 'invented citations' AI-failure line is added.
- Review fix applied: 'fiduciary' added to the no-relationship sentence; a no-endorsement-by-licensor line added to the licence paragraph.
- REJECTED: the reviewers' suggested 'none of them has prepared, reviewed or endorsed this work' (employer and AI-company makers). It asserts facts about third parties that cannot be known from the files; 'does not represent any employer or other organisation' and 'naming anyone implies no endorsement' do the job without inventing a fact.
- REJECTED: reviewer 2's holdings wording ('any positions, if there are any, may differ'). It hints at positions, which the hard constraints say to avoid. The holdings sentence is dropped entirely, so the text implies neither that the author holds positions nor that they do not.
- REJECTED: the 'has not been peer reviewed or audited' assertion. Whether any human review has happened is unknown, so it is not asserted. The owner should say whether to add it.
- Omitted for the word limit (not rejected on principle): the jurisdiction line ('not aimed at anyone where publishing would be unlawful'), which reviewer 2 allowed to drop; the 'Claude, Grok and Gemini named only as tools' sentence, dropped because it contradicted the README's 'work as peers'; the models-and-event-studies-are-simplifications line; the external-links caveat; and the 'this page adds no terms to the licence' line (unneeded once the imperative 'do not use it to decide' was removed).
- Not stated anywhere in the text, by design: the names of the author or employer, the author's regulatory or licensed status, whether the author holds positions, any claim that a lawyer drafted this, and any specific corrections count.
- Placement and follow-up edits for the owner, outside the disclaimer text: (1) put page_line in or directly under the 'What this page is, and what it is not' box in docs/index.html, with the absolute github.com blob link, and keep a one-line echo in the footer; (2) place readme_short as a short 'Disclaimer' section directly after 'Who does the work' in README.md; (3) change 'the principal' to 'the author' in docs/index.html (lines 211, 214, 674, 675, 728, 730) and GLOSSARY.md so the page does not carry two names for one person; (4) correct the README sentence 'Third-party data and paywalled text are summarised and cited, and they are not redistributed' to match the new text, because the repository tracks third-party files, 63 of them marked 'UNCERTAIN ... terms not read' in the manifest, and the owner should decide whether to keep those in a public repo; (5) check the employer's rules on outside publication, financial research and personal trading, which a disclaimer cannot satisfy; (6) CLAUDE.md line 537 ('No disclaimers') is an agent note and should not be public-facing.

## Review 1: verdict ship with fixes

- **blocking** — "Third-party data, text, names and trademarks belong to their owners. They are summarised and cited, not redistributed, and naming them implies no affiliation or endorsement.": False as a statement of fact about the repository. The repo's own data/THIRD_PARTY_MANIFEST.tsv lists 229 third-party files. 100 of them are tracked in git (checked with git ls-files): 63 are marked 'UNCERTAIN ... terms not read' (series caches, GPIF, NBIM, DNB/EIOPA/ECB tables), plus EIA, CFTC, IMF and SAFE files. _research/primary_sources also holds 30 tracked papers and extracts. The README says the same thing ('not redistributed'), and REPRODUCE.md says only Shiller, FRED DGS10/DFII10 and Damodaran are withheld. A public notice that is demonstrably untrue weakens the whole disclaimer, and it papers over a possible copyright exposure that is the owner's call. Fix: Replace with: 'Third-party data, text, names and trademarks belong to their owners and keep their own terms; CC BY 4.0 does not apply to them. Some third-party files are included so that results can be rebuilt; data/THIRD_PARTY_MANIFEST.tsv lists them with their source and what is known of their terms. If you own material here and object to its inclusion, open an issue. Naming a person, company or product implies no affiliation or endorsement.' Correct the README sentence to match. The owner should separately decide whether to keep the 63 'terms not read' files in a public repo.
- **should fix** — "Anything forward-looking here is a hypothesis, not a forecast.": Contradicted by the repo. Analysis/Forward_Prediction_Register.md is titled 'PRE-REGISTERED FORWARD PREDICTION REGISTER' and says 'Predict incumbent capture' and 'Klepper consolidation'. Analysis/Input_Substrate_Forward_Thinkers.md carries named investors' forward claims. Not everything forward-looking is graded HYPOTHESIS under the four-grade scheme. A reader can quote this sentence against a file that plainly makes predictions. Fix: 'Anything forward-looking here, including the pre-registered predictions in the repository, is speculative and may prove wrong. It is not a forecast or a recommendation.'
- **should fix** — "It has nothing to do with any employer of the author, past or present, or with any other organisation, and it does not represent their views.": Asserts unknown facts beyond the owner's instruction, which was 'my employer' only. 'Past', and 'any other organisation', are additions. The copyright and licence are held by the byline 'closeofbusiness', and if that is an entity the sentence contradicts the LICENSE. The project also plainly has something to do with Anthropic, xAI, Google and the data publishers. 'Nothing to do with' is a factual claim the owner may not be able to defend if knowledge from a job informed the work. Fix: 'This is published in a personal capacity. It does not represent the views of any employer or other organisation the author is or has been associated with, and none of them has prepared, reviewed or endorsed it.' Drop 'nothing to do with ... any other organisation'. The owner should confirm the employer sentence is true for them. They should also check their employer's outside-activity, publication and personal-trading rules, which a disclaimer cannot satisfy.
- **should fix** — "Everything here is the personal opinion of the author, the person who directs this project.": Internally inconsistent with the AI-authorship section ('AI models do most of the research and writing'). It also calls data and identities opinion: the page's Z.1 decompositions are arithmetic and measurement, not opinion. Someone challenging a claim can point out that the author did not write it. Fix: 'The views, analysis and conclusions published here are the author's own, published in a personal capacity, even where AI models drafted them. Figures taken from cited sources are those sources' figures.' This still delivers the owner's point 1.
- **should fix** — "Discussing an asset says nothing about anyone's holdings.": Reads as a non-sequitur and can be taken either as a denial of positions or as a dodge. It is not the recognised conflicts line, and it sits inside 'Not advice'. It does not assert anything unknown, but its meaning is unclear. Fix: 'Nothing here discloses what the author or anyone else holds or has an interest in. Do not infer holdings, or the absence of them, from what is or is not discussed.' Move it to its own short line, or drop it.
- **should fix** — "To the fullest extent the law allows, the author accepts no liability for any loss or damage arising from its use or from reliance on it.": Wrong party and too narrow. LICENSE and README put the copyright and byline in 'closeofbusiness', and CC BY 4.0 s.5 disclaims on behalf of the 'Licensor'. The candidate protects only 'the author', not the byline, contributors (the repo is public) or anyone hosting it. The later 'author endorses your use' sentence should likewise track the licence's s.2(a)(6), which speaks of the Licensor. Fix: '... the author, anyone publishing under the closeofbusiness byline, and any contributor accept no liability ...'. In the Licence section, say the licence gives no permission to imply endorsement by the licensor or the author (CC BY 4.0, section 2(a)(6)).
- **should fix** — "Third-party data, text, names and trademarks belong to their owners.": No clause covers statements about named people, companies or publications, which the repo is full of. Analysis/Input_Substrate_Forward_Thinkers.md reports named investors' views, including a quantified 'Oracle earnings overstated ~27%' attributed to one of them. The page discusses Oracle, SpaceX and Apple. The Singh notes dispute a named economist's numbers. Paid-newsletter cards summarise a named writer. This is the main defamation and misattribution exposure of an AI-written record, and it is not addressed. Fix: Add: 'Statements about named companies, people or publications come from cited public sources, were processed by AI models, and may be wrong or out of date. A summary of what someone said is not a statement by them. Where this work disagrees with a published result, it disputes the number or the argument, not the person.' Add the issue route for corrections.
- **should fix** — "Views and conclusions may change without notice, and the author has no duty to update this material.": Omits dates, vintages and copies. The page is a 'state of play at 14 October 2026'. Z.1 figures are quarterly and revised. Git history, forks and archives preserve withdrawn claims (the README admits CORRECTIONS.md exists because a retracted claim 'kept publishing' for four days). The candidate dropped the 'check the date' line for length. CC BY 4.0 invites copies that will never see a correction. Fix: Add: 'Figures are as at the dates stated and are not updated in real time. Older versions, forks and copies may contain claims since withdrawn; check the current version and CORRECTIONS.md.'
- **should fix** — "Full disclaimer: DISCLAIMER.md in the repository.": Not a link, and a relative link would fail. The page is served from docs/, so docs/DISCLAIMER.md does not exist on Pages. The page's other repo links all use absolute github.com/.../blob/main/ URLs. Footer placement is also weak: this should sit next to the 'What this page is' box, not only in the footer. That box says 'It is written by AI models for the principal', and the page uses 'principal' three more times, so the new 'author' wording will read as a second person. Fix: Link https://github.com/closeofbusiness/market-plumbing/blob/main/DISCLAIMER.md. Place the line in or directly under the 'What this page is' box. Change 'principal' to 'author' on the public page in the same edit.
- **should fix** — "The work is published purely for academic interest and entertainment.": Tension with the README and page, which call the page 'the authoritative overview', 'ruling of 14 October 2026'. A reader or adversary can read 'authoritative' as a claim of correctness next to an 'entertainment only' disclaimer. The candidate never explains the term. Fix: Add one sentence in the errors section: ''Authoritative' in this project means which document wins when two differ. It is not a claim that the content is correct.' Keep the owner's 'academic and entertainment' wording as requested.
- **minor** — "[CORRECTIONS.md](../CORRECTIONS.md) records more than a hundred claims already withdrawn, evidence that errors occur and are corrected.": Slightly inaccurate. CORRECTIONS.md has 145 entries, but some correct earlier corrections (C-058 was itself retracted by C-059) rather than withdraw a claim. It also misses the best evidence of the specific AI failure: C-079 is 'a paper that does not exist'. The relative link will break if the text is reused on the Pages site. Fix: '... records more than a hundred corrections, including figures that were wrong, corrections that were themselves wrong, and a cited paper that does not exist.' Use an absolute URL if the text is reused on the page.
- **minor** — "Do not use it to decide what to do with money.": An imperative field-of-use restriction sits awkwardly with CC BY 4.0, which grants rights for any purpose and forbids the licensor from adding further restrictions. It also sits oddly beside 'do your own research ... take advice', which tells people to decide, just not on this alone. Fix: 'It is not a suitable basis for decisions about money.' This states a fact about the work and imposes no term.
- **minor** — "The project's own write-ups are licensed under [CC BY 4.0](../LICENSE).": Silent on scope. The repo has 44 scripts (bin/) and many derived data tables. GitHub reports the whole repo as cc-by-4.0, while the README limits the licence to 'write-ups'. The disclaimer should say what the as-is warranty exclusion covers. Fix: 'Scripts and derived data tables in the repository are also provided as is. Third-party files keep their own terms.' The owner decides whether to extend CC BY to code.

Missing elements named: A rights-holder and corrections route. GitHub issues are enabled on the repo (gh repo view reports hasIssuesEnabled true). Say where to report errors and where a rights owner can ask for removal. This pairs with the redistribution fix.; A statement that the work has not been peer reviewed or audited. The 'outside reviews' are by Grok and Gemini, which are AI models. Confirm with the owner before asserting it.; An AI-specific failure line: models can invent citations, misread filings and state wrong figures with confidence. 'Make mistakes' undersells it, and C-079 shows an invented paper in the record.; A one-line caveat that models, counterfactuals, event studies and sizing exercises (P2, P3, RV1) are simplifications and not predictions. Standard in research disclaimers.; The word 'fiduciary' or 'advisory' in the no-relationship sentence. 'No adviser-client or other professional relationship' is adequate but is not the phrase readers or regulators look for.; A short external-links sentence: third-party sites and papers are not controlled or endorsed.; Outside the disclaimer text, for the owner. The repo is not anonymous. 42 tracked files name the owner by first name, commits are authored 'MartinS', and THE_ASK.md and the page quote the director's own rulings under the label 'principal'. A 'personal capacity' notice does not discharge employer rules on outside publishing, financial research or personal trading. The disclaimer deliberately says nothing on regulated status or holdings, which is right, but the owner should check those separately.; Also outside the disclaimer text. The README repeats the 'not redistributed' claim. The README short block, page line and full file should all be corrected together, and CLAUDE.md line 537 ('No disclaimers') is a note to agents and should not be public-facing.

## Review 2: verdict ship with fixes

- **should fix** — "Everything here is the personal opinion of the author, the person who directs this project. (and, in the short versions) "the author" / "its author"": Vocabulary collision. The README says "AI models do the research and the writing" and "The byline is closeofbusiness"; the licence holder is closeofbusiness; the live page (docs/index.html lines 211, 214, 674-675, 728, 730) and GLOSSARY.md row 9 call the same person "the principal". The disclaimer adds a third name, "the author", and a reader of the README will take "author" to mean the AI. The README short and page line are meant to stand alone, so they cannot rely on a definition that only appears in the long file. Fix: Define it once in the long file: "This project is published under the byline closeofbusiness. In this disclaimer, 'the author' means the person who directs it." In the README short and page line use "the person who directs this project" rather than "its author". In the same change, replace "the principal" in the page (lines 211, 214, 674, 675, 728, 730) and GLOSSARY.md with the same plain term. Otherwise the new line sits two paragraphs from a different name for the same person.
- **should fix** — "It has nothing to do with any employer of the author, past or present, or with any other organisation, and it does not represent their views.": Overbroad and partly untrue as written. The owner asked for "my employer". "Nothing to do with ... any other organisation" is false on its face: the work is published by closeofbusiness, built with Claude (Anthropic), Grok (xAI) and Gemini (Google) as the README says, and drawn from the Fed, SEC and others. "Past or present" also goes beyond the request. A cold reader will notice the overreach. Fix: "It has nothing to do with the author's employer, and it does not represent the views of any employer, past or present, or of any other organisation, including the companies that make the AI models used." Keep "nothing to do with" for the employer only and use "does not represent the views of" for everyone else.
- **should fix** — "Discussing an asset says nothing about anyone's holdings.": Cryptic, and the one defensive aside in the file. It sits at the end of the not-advice paragraph with no setup, and "anyone's holdings" does not say whose. It is neutral on positions, as the constraint requires, but it reads as a hint at something. Fix: Either drop it, or say it plainly in its own sentence: "Nothing here discloses what the author or anyone else holds, and any positions, if there are any, may differ from the views expressed."
- **should fix** — "Do not use it to decide what to do with money.": Two issues. (1) It is an imperative restriction on use. CC BY 4.0 (s.2(a)(5)(B)) bars the licensor from adding terms that restrict the licensed use, so the licence section should say this page adds none. (2) It overlaps the next section, which tells the reader to take their own advice before deciding, and it is blunter than the rest of the file. Fix: "It is not a basis for deciding what to do with money." Add one line to the Licence section: "This page is a notice. It adds no terms to the licence and restricts nothing the licence allows."
- **minor** — "It is not directed at anyone where publishing or using it would be unlawful.": The most legalese line in the file, and ambiguous about who is publishing or using. "Using it would be unlawful" also reads like a use restriction, which raises the same CC BY problem as above. It is the line a front-page reader would call boilerplate. Fix: "It is not aimed at anyone in a place where publishing it would be unlawful." Or drop it and rely on the 'as is' and no-liability section, since the owner asked only for the standard items.
- **should fix** — "AI models do most of the research and writing, directed by the author. / Most of the analysis is produced by AI models": The README says "AI models do the research and the writing" and "One person directs the work". The candidate says "most", and the short version says "Most of the analysis", so the three texts disagree on how much is AI. It also strains against "Everything here is the personal opinion of the author": if AI writes it, whose opinion is it? Fix: Pick one wording and use it in the README, disclaimer, shorts and page. Preferably keep the README's: "AI models do the research and the writing. The person who directs the work sets the question and the standard of evidence, and publishes it as a personal view." If "largely" stays, change the README line to match.
- **minor** — "Claude, Grok and Gemini are named only as tools.": The README and GLOSSARY.md say Claude and Grok "work as peers". "Only as tools" reads as a contradiction on the same front page, and as a slight dismissal of Gemini, which the README describes as consulted for reading and research. Fix: "Claude, Grok and Gemini are named only to say which models were used."
- **minor** — "[CORRECTIONS.md](../CORRECTIONS.md) records more than a hundred claims already withdrawn, evidence that errors occur and are corrected. Assume more remain.": Two issues. The sentence is a comma-spliced run-on, against the 'short declarative sentences' register of the README. And "more than a hundred" understates the figure the page banner shows in plain view (145 registered corrections, C-001 to C-145), so it looks coy next to the real number. Fix: "CORRECTIONS.md is the register of every claim withdrawn so far. That is evidence that errors occur and are corrected. Assume more remain." This uses no count, so it cannot go stale.
- **should fix** — "Full disclaimer: DISCLAIMER.md in the repository.": On the rendered Pages site a bare filename is not a link, and a relative link would 404 because Pages serves only docs/ (docs/index.html is its only file). The existing footer links every repository file by absolute github.com blob URL, in `<span class="num">` styling, with " · " separators. The line is also fragment-heavy ("Not investment, legal, accounting or tax advice; academic and entertainment purposes only."), and the footer is the quietest place on the page. The existing 'What this page is, and what it is not' box is the natural home. Fix: Put two full sentences in that box, with an absolute link to https://github.com/closeofbusiness/market-plumbing/blob/main/DISCLAIMER.md. For example: "This page is the personal view of the person who directs the project and has nothing to do with any employer. It is not investment, legal, accounting or tax advice, it is published for academic and entertainment purposes, and because AI models wrote it, errors are expected. Full disclaimer: DISCLAIMER.md." Keep a one-line footer echo in the footer's existing format.
- **minor** — "The project's own write-ups are licensed under CC BY 4.0. The licence's disclaimer of warranties and limitation of liability apply in addition to this page. The licence gives no permission to suggest ": Accurate (s.2(a)(6) no endorsement, s.5 disclaimer), but three small inconsistencies. The README says "The repository's own write-ups", not "project's". The licensor in LICENSE is closeofbusiness, not 'the author', which is the mapping gap flagged above. The last sentence suddenly addresses the re-user as 'you' in a section otherwise addressed to the reader. Fix: "The repository's own write-ups are © closeofbusiness and licensed under CC BY 4.0. - **minor** — "## Academic and entertainment purposes / ## Risk / ## Third parties / ## Changes (ten H2 sections, 404 words)": Length is fine for a linked file, since it is about a one-minute read and the shorts do the front-page work. But ten headed micro-sections of one to three sentences each look like a form, against the README's five-section prose. Nothing is wrong in substance, but the structure reads as boilerplate. Fix: Merge to about six sections: (1) Whose views these are; (2) Not advice, with the purpose line and the risk sentences; (3) Get your own advice; (4) Largely written by AI, and sometimes wrong; (5) No warranty, no liability, with Changes and the jurisdiction line; (6) Third parties and licence.
- **should fix** — "Deliberately omitted: ... any 'check the date on each document' line (dropped for the word limit)": The omitted line matters for this repository. The README says the page is the authoritative overview. A reader who lands on an old note and takes it as the current view is the likeliest real misreading. The word limit applies to the shorts, not to the long file. Fix: Add to 'Changes': "The overview page is the current position. The repository also keeps dated working notes, some superseded and some preserved as a record of withdrawn claims. Check the date on any note and CORRECTIONS.md before relying on it."
