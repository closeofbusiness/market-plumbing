# N3R_Velocity_Review_Return.md

**Verdict:** VULNERABLE — Singh's own January 2026 update keeps global pledged collateral at roughly USD 13trn and velocity near 2.0 for the past decade; the programme's USD 1.5 estimate rests on a Form PF all-collateral bracket proxy Singh does not use and that his published round pair implicitly rejects.

**Attacks:**

1. **[4, 5] · Singh's January 2026 headline pair contradicts the conclusion.** In Risk.net (12 Jan 2026, free to read), Singh reports a global pledged pool of roughly USD 13trn, JP Morgan and Barclays each near USD 1.8trn, US banks over half the global market, and collateral velocity that "has remained near 2.0 for the past decade." That is the metric's author, on the same pledged total the programme uses, explicitly rejecting a fall to ~1.5. The programme treats Singh's 13 / 2.0 as stale only after its PF bracket yields 6.9trn for the hedge-fund leg alone — but Singh's round numbers already encode a total source stock near 6.5trn, i.e. a lower hedge-fund share than the bracket carry-forward, not a lower velocity. **Direction:** the 6.9trn hedge-fund estimate is biased **up** by ~USD 1.4-1.9trn relative to what Singh's 13 / 2.0 pair implies. **Settle with:** Singh's unpublished source split for 2025-26, or a reconciled table from his hand-collected bank set matching the January 2026 charts.

2. **[2] · Bracket carry-forward is unidentified and fails a bound test.** The 0.60 mean position is a free parameter fit on 2013-2017 overlap; nothing in Form PF or Singh ties his prime-brokerage-centric "source collateral" to a fixed point between securities-only and all-collateral posted. OFR API (verified 28 Aug 2026): 2026Q1 securities posted USD 4.92trn, all posted USD 8.21trn. At position 0.60 that yields USD 6.90trn; at position 0 (securities only) it yields USD 4.92trn. With Singh's global numerator USD 13trn and a real-money leg of USD 1.5trn, velocity at position 0 is 13 / (4.92 + 1.5) ≈ **2.0** — exactly Singh's headline. The 0.60 extrapolation across the basis-trade / zero-haircut Treasury era has no structural anchor. **Direction:** bracket choice swings the hedge-fund leg by ~USD 3.3trn (up if forced to 1.0, down if forced to 0). **Settle with:** prime-brokerage-only collateral field from Form PF Q43 counterparty breakdown (prime broker vs repo vs other secured), not the type aggregate.

3. **[2, 1] · Form PF Q43 "all collateral posted" is not Singh's hedge-fund source.** OFR defines Q43 all collateral to include cash, cash equivalents, US Treasury and agency securities posted when borrowing **securities**, plus letters of credit (`financialresearch.gov/hedge-fund-monitor`, chart-34 methodology). Singh's source bucket is title-transfer collateral entering dealer reuse chains from prime brokerage, repo, and uncleared derivatives — not every margin posting. Wedges: (a) **cash/cash-equivalent leg** USD 2.92trn of USD 8.21trn in 2026Q1 (OFR API `FPF-COLLATERALTYPE_CASH_COLLATERAL_SUM`) — much is stock-lending margin, not rehypothecatable supply; **inflates** if counted at face value (order USD 1-2trn, UNCERTAIN split). (b) **CCP / cleared IM** — Q43 other secured and cleared derivatives lines include FCM-posted initial margin that never hits dealer "permitted to repledge" footnotes; ISDA Margin Survey year-end 2025: USD 423.5bn required IM to major CCPs for rates/CDS globally, CCP Global Q4 2025: USD 1.52trn global IM required across all products — hedge-fund share UNCERTAIN, likely **inflates** by low hundreds of bn USD. (c) **Qualifying hedge fund perimeter** — OFR uses only QHFs (≥USD 500m net assets, large advisers); Singh's pre-2018 estimates likely covered a wider "hedge fund" label — **deflates** modestly (UNCERTAIN magnitude). (d) **Over-collateralization excess** — OFR OCR 13.3% in 2026Q1 (`FPF-OCRATEALL_PERCENT`); total borrowing USD 7.25trn vs posted USD 8.21trn implies ~USD 0.97trn posted in excess of debt — not primary source for chains; **inflates** ~USD 1.0trn. (e) **Double count with sec-lending** — hedge funds borrow on-loan securities via sec-lending and also appear as Q43 collateral posters; ISLA on-loan includes those flows while Singh's sec-lending leg is pension/insurer supply — **inflates** denominator if both legs capture HF activity (UNCERTAIN, low hundreds of bn). (f) **Churn asymmetry** — ISLA EUR 3.9trn on-loan (31 Mar 2026) includes roll churn Singh excludes from sources; using it as an upper bound is safe, treating it as measured source is not. **Settle with:** Form PF Q43 counterparty-type split (prime broker borrowing vs repo vs cleared) cross-walked to a single dealer's prime-brokerage footnote.

4. **[2] · Basis-trade era should move the bracket position toward 1.0, not lock 0.60 — but that strengthens pass-through, not lengthening chains.** Post-2019 Treasury RV and basis trades increased secured borrowing and securities collateral simultaneously. OFR: securities posted rose from USD 1.82trn (2017Q4) to USD 4.92trn (2026Q1); repo borrowing from USD 1.05trn to USD 3.24trn; prime-broker borrowing USD 1.05trn to USD 3.22trn. OCR was already ~17% in 2017Q4 vs 13.3% in 2026Q1 (computed from OFR borrowing sums). More collateral per unit borrow is consistent with **more** all-collateral weight, i.e. bracket position **up** toward 1.0 — which **inflates** the 6.9trn estimate further. The programme interprets parallel growth in dealer footnotes and HF collateral as pass-through (velocity flat/falling); that is coherent only if the **numerator** scales with primary sources. Applying a rising bracket to a rising all-collateral series compounds source growth faster than Singh's flat 2.0 allows. **Direction:** if position drifted 0.60 → 0.75, hedge-fund source ~USD 7.4trn, pushing velocity below 1.5 — but that contradicts Singh, suggesting the bracket method is broken, not that velocity fell. **Settle with:** time-varying bracket estimated from prime-broker-only Q43 fields, 2018-2026.

5. **[3, 6] · Numerator/denominator perimeter is half-global, half-local, and biasing when misaligned.** Singh's velocity uses ~10-15 global dealer banks' pledged total (numerator) over **global** primary sources (denominator). The programme mixes: global numerator USD 13trn (Singh Jan 2026), global PF hedge-fund proxy, ISLA **global** on-loan EUR 3.9trn, but validates growth from a **US-six** footnote sum ~USD 6.9trn (JPM USD 1.77trn + GS USD 1.31trn + MS USD 1.19trn + Citi USD 1.06trn + BofA USD 1.10trn ≈ USD 6.44trn from Dec 2025 10-K/10-Q footnotes; UNCERTAIN sixth name to reach 6.9). Singh states US banks > half of USD 13trn; the five-name sum is ~50%. If European dealers (Barclays global USD 1.31trn, Deutsche, UBS) carry a disproportionate share of **non-US** primary sources while the PF hedge-fund leg is US-heavy, using a US-inflated HF source against a global pledged pool **depresses** velocity artificially. Conversely, if the real-money leg is bounded by **global** ISLA EUR 3.9trn (~USD 4.2-4.5trn at market FX, UNCERTAIN) rather than Singh's USD 1.5trn 2017 US-centric leg, denominator rises and velocity falls further — but that bound includes churn and non-dealer-bank borrowers, violating Singh's source definition. **Direction:** perimeter mismatch can swing velocity ±0.3-0.5 either way depending which leg is widened. **Settle with:** geographically matched numerator (global 10-15 bank footnote sum) and denominator (Singh-method hedge funds + custodian sec-lending to dealers only).

6. **[1, 6] · Pass-through vs chain-lengthening is misread from level co-movement.** The programme cites FR 2004C reuse intensity flat-to-falling since 2023 and parallel +24% y/y growth in dealer collateral-received vs hedge-fund secured borrowing. OFR Brief 24-07 (2024) documents dealers rehypothecating ~65% of reverse-repo collateral — high pass-through is **consistent with velocity ~2**, not proof it fell to 1.5. ECB working paper 3147 / SUERF (2025): euro repo reuse rate ~11.6%, average chain length ~2.95 — in line with Singh's pre-crisis chain-length interpretation (~3 in 2007), not compression to 1.5. **Direction:** neutral to **upward** on true velocity vs 1.5 claim. **Settle with:** Singh-style chain-length estimate on US dealer footnotes 2023-2026, not FR 2004C alone.

**Alternative reading (task D):**

Singh's January 2026 USD 13trn / ~2.0 pair is the authoritative reconciliation: pledged collateral rose while reuse intensity stayed near 2.0 because **both** primary sources and the pledged pool grew roughly in proportion — not because chains shortened to 1.5. The arithmetic tension the programme flags (13 / 2.0 ⇒ sources ~6.5trn, below the USD 6.9trn hedge-fund leg alone) is evidence that the **PF bracket carry-forward overshoots** Singh's hedge-fund partition, not that velocity collapsed. If hedge-fund sources are nearer USD 5.0-5.5trn (consistent with Singh's implied total and with securities-plus-prime-broker posting rather than all Q43 collateral) and the real-money leg remains USD 1.5-2.0trn, velocity stays ~2.0. Flat measured velocity alongside rising levels is also compatible with Singh's "digital curtain" warning: intraday tokenised collateral (JP Morgan Kinexys, cited in the same January 2026 piece) may leave **true** reuse **above** the end-of-day 2.0 the programme tries to push below — making USD 1.5 a double artefact of proxy error and EoD under-counting.
