## Method note on this table

"Files" counts are any-mention (a file tagged channels `b,c` counts toward both). Primary-channel-only
counts are given in parentheses for a–d because several large synthesis documents (`Shadow_Debt_Channel_Map.md`,
`2026-08-29-N2c-The-Funding-Closure.md`, the N2cR review pair) legitimately span three or four channels
at once and would otherwise be invisible from a primary-only count. "3 most substantial" is ranked by
file size in bytes (an objective, reproducible proxy), cross-checked against my qualitative read of
centrality during classification — in every channel the size-ranked top file matched the document I
would independently call the load-bearing one for that channel.
