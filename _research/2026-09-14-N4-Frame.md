## The question as it stands, and why it is now dead

N4 has been carried as: *"how much of the compression, or of the funding behind equity purchases, ran
through nonbank money and collateral re-use."*

**That form is unanswerable, and yesterday's work is what killed it.** Tracing a funding channel to a
purchase requires knowing who purchased. We established that **94.6% of net new equity was absorbed by a
sector the Fed computes as a residual**, whose named contents include private equity funds and personal
trusts, and which cannot be split by wealth group, institution or buyer type on free data
(`2026-09-14-HR-The-Household-Residual.md`). **You cannot trace a dollar to a buyer you cannot identify.**

Any answer of the form "X% of the funding ran through the collateral channel" would therefore be
manufactured. Registering that now, so the number is never produced.

## The restatement — three questions that ARE answerable

Drop tracing. Ask about **scale, timing and capacity** instead. This is the same move that makes the
money-creation question tractable, and it should be applied consistently:

**N4a — Did it expand?** Over 2024:Q1–2026:Q2, did the nonbank money and collateral system grow, and by how
much? Components already built and sitting unjoined: source collateral and velocity
(`2026-08-31-N3v4-Singh-Reconciliation.md`), the wholesale share z_k
(`2026-08-31-N2b-zk-The-Wholesale-Share.md`), the offshore dollar leg
(`2026-08-30-N2a-Offshore-Dollar-Leg.md`), and the Treasury absorption refresh
(`2026-09-12-N2c-Closure-Refresh-2026Q2.md`).

**N4b — Was the expansion large enough to have funded the purchases?** A scale comparison against the
$3,243.1bn of net new equity. This sets an **upper bound** and nothing more — but an upper bound is a real
result, and if the channel is an order of magnitude too small that closes the question outright.

**N4c — Did the timing match?** The premium compression is front-loaded into 2024 and has since partly
reversed. If nonbank money and collateral expanded in a different year, the channel is not the explanation
for the thing we are trying to explain, whatever its size.

## The outcome that must stay available

**"These channels cannot be connected to the equity price question with free data, and here is the
structural reason" is a legitimate and possibly correct answer.** It is not a failure to be worked around.

If that is where this lands, it should be written up as **a limit of the programme** rather than left as an
open item that quietly never closes — because an open item nobody can close is worse than a closed one with
a reason. The goal names shadow banking and the collateral channel explicitly, so if they are unreachable,
that belongs in the answer, plainly stated.

## What this is NOT

- Not a multiplier. Nothing here converts a collateral stock into a price move (C-077, C-080).
- Not an attribution of the residual. That is settled as unattributable; do not reopen it here.
- Not a re-derivation of the collateral panel, which is built and adversarially reviewed.
