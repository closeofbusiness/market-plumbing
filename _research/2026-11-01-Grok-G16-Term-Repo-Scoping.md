The statistic is that volume-weighted mean minus the same-source overnight/open mean on the quarter’s last business day, set against the same spread on other business days that month which are not themselves month-ends, with transaction volume beside it so a suppressed print is not read as a calm day. Compare quarter-ends from September 2025 onward with one another, including the June 2026, September 2026, and December 2026 turns the page already frames with overnight prints. Keep any LE30 exercise inside dates through 12 Aug 2025, with no level comparison across the join.
