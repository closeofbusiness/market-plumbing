BOUND VERDICT: fails — as an unconditional finding that “flows cannot be the whole story.”

The 13.47 figure divides revaluation (+$43,676.3bn) by Z.1 *net* flow (+$3,243.1bn). That denominator is market-clearing net issuance, not the demand shift M is defined on (Gabaix–Koijen portfolio shift into equities). Gross buying-sector purchases were $6,886.8bn → implied M = 6.34, inside the published 1.5–9.4 envelope, so the envelope can cover the entire repricing (C-080). Both denominators remain equilibrium quantities. A second mismatch stands: Z.1’s $123.7trn stock mixes listed and closely-held while published Ms are listed-market objects; ETF1 shows issuance net of ETF shares is −$360.7bn in the same window — the “flow” that matched the household plug was mostly the ETF wrapper, not new operating-company equity.

CORRECT DENOMINATOR: the exogenous *demand shift into listed equities* (cash/bond-financed portfolio entry, or a mechanical mandate flow), never Z.1 net issuance or gross sector purchases; for accounting ceilings only, net new listed supply is a conditional upper bound on how much *issuance-constrained* flow can explain.

AGGREGATION CLAIM: partly — stock-level elasticities do not rescale to the market when the counterparty is another equity (substitution / within-class rotation); P4’s free Russell RD never assigned treatment and cannot be rescaled (C-086). But “only boundary-crossing flows matter” is too strong: Jiang–Vayanos–Zheng (RFS 2025; PDF `personal.lse.ac.uk/vayanos/Papers/PIRMF_RFSf.pdf`, checked in B0 13 Sep) show the aggregate can rise from active→passive switching alone via mega-firm idiosyncratic-risk amplification.

Flows that *do* cross the asset-class boundary and are measurable from free sources: (1) foreign net purchases of US equities — Treasury TIC monthly (https://home.treasury.gov/data/treasury-international-capital-tic-system, fetched 2026-09-15); (2) net new equity supply net of buybacks — Fed Z.1 F51 / ETF1 strip (https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/z1/, vintage 2026-09-11); (3) retirement contributions net of withdrawals — ICI + DOL Form 5500 aggregates where browser-reachable; (4) NBIM petroleum inflows allocated at strategic equity weights — NBIM annual/quarterly reports in `data/bridge_firststage/nbim/`; (5) cash-financed LETF *creations* (not daily rebalance, which needs no new cash) — issuer AUM/holdings CSVs (Direxion/ProShares, 2026-09-15).

THE DESIGN: **Dividend-payment-timing IV** (Hartzmark–Solomon marketwide payment-day pressure; AER 2025 reports market-level M ≈ 1.9; NBER w30688 / Bocconi PDF give 1.5–2.3 — https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.20231725 and https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w30688/w30688.pdf, resolved 2026-09-15).

VARIATION: day-to-day variation in predictable cash hitting equity holders on *payment* dates (legally obligated; typically announced ~3–6 weeks earlier), which must be consumed, parked in cash, or reinvested — a marketwide, price-insensitive flow that is not within-equity rotation.

INSTRUMENT: aggregate payment yield = Σ(cash dividends paid on day t across listed payers) / lagged listed market cap; build amounts and payment dates from free SEC EDGAR/XBRL dividend tags in 8-K/10-Q/10-K (`us-gaap_DividendsPayableDateToBePaidDayMonthAndYear` and related; https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) plus Nasdaq/Yahoo dividend calendars; market cap and value-weighted returns from Stooq/Yahoo free daily closes (same stack P4 already used).

SAMPLE: US common stocks with free daily prices, daily frequency, longest window free data support (target ~1990–2026); primary object is the value-weighted market return on high vs low payment-yield days; report quintile and continuous specifications.

THREAT: payment-day clustering correlated with macro news or firm-size composition; free-price series lack CRSP delisting discipline (attenuation/noise); the identified object is a *days-horizon temporary price-pressure multiplier*, not a permanent multi-year M on Z.1 lines; reinvestment intensity varies with mutual-fund distribution policy.

FALSIFICATION: (i) null or much weaker effect on announcement and ex-dates once payment-day yield is controlled; (ii) effect larger on high-VIX days and when mutual-fund reinvestment is high (HS’s own heterogeneity); (iii) placebo: randomly assigned same-dollar non-dividend cash days should not move the market the same way; (iv) kill switch — if high-minus-low payment-day return gap is indistinguishable from zero after seasonality and VIX controls, the free-data replication fails and do not invent an M.

SECOND-BEST DESIGN: **JVZ mega-firm cross-section on free data** — regress size-sorted listed returns and idiosyncratic volatility on equity-ETF flow shocks measured from free N-PORT holdings of full-replication or large equity ETFs (IVV/SPY-class) plus ICI/Z.1 equity-ETF issuance (ETF1), testing whether passive *within-equity* flows raise mega-firm prices enough to lift the aggregate. This does not deliver a scalar M for the household plug, but it is the direct free-data test of the mechanism that amends the aggregation claim, and it uses files the project already holds. Do not revive the sovereign-mandate band IV as primary: B5 found GPIF’s foreign-equity band never bound in 25 quarters and NBIM’s monthly trigger is unobservable; the inflow fallback had F=3.41 on n=9 (`data/bridge_firststage/`). Under E-005 that slope (+1.08) is a weak first-stage fact, not an identification strategy for reduced-form M until monthly mandate flows are published.

CAN IT BE DONE?: only-for-a-subset — days-horizon LATE for predictable dividend-cash reinvestment into the listed market (and, separately, a mechanism test for mega-firm passive amplification); not a scalar M for the Z.1 household residual, not a multi-year explanation of the 2024–26 +$43.7trn revaluation, and not a reconstitution LATE after C-086.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE YOUR ANSWER: free monthly NBIM benchmark-basis equity weights / GPIF mandate trade prints, or free official Russell May float ranks and full-replication membership, would reopen a multi-year mandate or index IV; until then, only the payment-day and JVZ designs are honest free-data aggregate routes.
