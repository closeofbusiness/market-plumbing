# Long-money holders

## Hypothesis

The marginal buyer of equity, of duration, and of AI-related debt is long money (insurers, pensions, mutual funds that are not cash pools), as opposed to leveraged money or newly created deposits.

## Status

**Sized at sector level for corporate bonds; for the AI names, measured only for registered funds (one deal) and money funds (a name search), so HYPOTHESIS (C-139). Open for duration and for commodity paper.** The equity holder table is a separate measurement and is mostly a wrapper-and-residual result. See the passive dossier.

## Evidence

At security level, money funds hold little of the AI debt that could be seen in filings; whether long money holds most of it is not measured for the AI names (C-139). Source: [`2026-08-22-D2-Who-Holds-The-AI-Paper.md`](../2026-08-22-D2-Who-Holds-The-AI-Paper.md).

Later composition work (W2): money funds hold about **$3.4bn** under AI names (about $4.25bn with Apple; a name-search floor, C-138), mostly as repo collateral that finances dealers' inventories of AI bonds rather than the companies (direct commercial paper is about $0.26bn; C-120), the $4.25bn with Apple is about **0.35%** of a roughly **$1.2trn** analyst estimate of AI-company-issued debt. The **$1.2trn** is an estimate, not a filing total. Large-bank C&I exposure to AI-adjacent industries is about **$450bn** committed and **$150bn** outstanding, but a similar classification was already about **$250bn** of commitments in 2015, so the build-out is an unmeasured part of that stock (C-115). Source: [`2026-09-22-W2-AI-Paper-Composition.md`](../2026-09-22-W2-AI-Paper-Composition.md).

Pensions were net sellers of the equity instrument over 2024:Q1–2026:Q2 on directly reported lines. That does not say they sold AI bonds.

## What would change the conclusion

No free census allocates the 2026 hyperscaler deals by investor type; order books are published, allocations are not (Grok K4,
13 Oct, in `_research/2026-10-13-Grok-Wave-Reviews-Return.md`). Insurer holdings by issuer (NAIC Schedule D) are not free. A free census that puts a large share of the AI paper in cash pools or in leveraged holders. The 2026:Q3 N-PORT pass ran on 9 October (Beignet flat at about $9.7bn, 36% of the deal in registered funds); the next is the 2026:Q4 bulk file. A duration holder table on the same basis does not exist yet.

## Data used

N-PORT and 10-K holdings in D2 and W2; Z.1 pension equity lines; money-fund holdings of named paper.
