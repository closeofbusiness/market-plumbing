# Wholesale repo and shadow money

## Hypothesis

Shadow-money creation — money-market funds, private repo, ABCP, stablecoins, offshore dollars — was the source of the equity bid, in the Pozsar–Singh sense that nonbank money did the work of base money.

## Status

**Failed as the 2024 source of the equity bid. Re-test** as a ceiling over the full 2024–26 window. The negative is first-class.

## Evidence

In 2024 the increase in wholesale repo came out of the Federal Reserve’s reverse-repo facility. Net of that handoff, private repo was about **−$46bn**. The handoff-adjusted ceiling for 2024 is about **$136bn**, against about **$1,405bn** of household purchases of the wrapper that year. Source: `2026-09-15-N4-Scale-Timing-Bound.md` (C-085).

Over the full window the source answer does not rule the channel out (C-118): raw wholesale growth is the same order of magnitude as a slice of purchases, and even after the handoff a quarter of purchases remains as a ceiling. No share of equity purchases is attributed to nonbank money. That number would be manufactured.

The wholesale share of nonbank funding of banks and dealers, `z_k`, rose and plateaued. Version 1’s headline was an endpoint artefact (C-070). Current reading is the v2 note: `2026-08-31-N2b-zk-The-Wholesale-Share.md`. Monitor: `zk_v2_W2_M2_pct` about **23%** at March 2026.

Stablecoins are a fifth cash pool in the taxonomy (`2026-08-29-D6-Stablecoins-Fifth-Cash-Pool.md`), not an equity-bid result. Outstanding in the monitor: about **$309bn** at 29 August 2026, from a free on-chain aggregate.

## What would change the conclusion

A wholesale-funding increase that is not a handoff from the reverse-repo facility, that lines up in time with the equity purchases, and that survives a wrapper strip. The OFR hedge-fund repo print for 2026:Q2 was still unpublished at the 24 September check (last observation 31 March 2026).

## Data used

OFR money-fund monitor, FRED reverse repo, OFR repo participant totals, Form PF (through 2026:Q1), ABCP outstanding, stablecoin float. See the monitor.
