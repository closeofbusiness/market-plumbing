# Fundamentals benchmark

## Hypothesis

Earnings, rates, and risk premia explain the equity move first. A liquidity or flow story has to beat that benchmark, not replace it. For commodities the benchmark is the physical balance and the inventory path. That commodity sentence is the programme brief; it is not yet tied to a source file.

## Status

**Sized** for the recent US equity window, and still a hypothesis under the charter. **Open** for rates and commodities.

## Evidence

- Earnings share **71.0% to December 2025 and 82.3% to June 2026** [MEASURED]. Source: [`2026-09-11-P2a-Return-Decomposition.md`](../2026-09-11-P2a-Return-Decomposition.md), as carried in the 17 September answer.
- Real yields **+0.34 points**, earnings yield **−0.14**, December 2023 to June 2026 [MEASURED]. The multiple held. Source: [`2026-09-12-P2c-Rates-vs-Risk-Premium.md`](../2026-09-12-P2c-Rates-vs-Risk-Premium.md).
- Per-share accretion is at most about a tenth to a sixth of the price gain since 2015 [BOUNDED]. Source: [`2026-09-18-EPS-Split.md`](../2026-09-18-EPS-Split.md).
- Useful-life extensions are **5–11%** of six megacaps’ net-income growth. Source: [`2026-09-13-E2-Earnings-Quality-Useful-Lives.md`](../2026-09-13-E2-Earnings-Quality-Useful-Lives.md).
- Tax: aggregate effective rate **27.4% to 19.6%** on 412 S&P 500 firms; restating 2025 earnings at the 2015 rate moves the earnings share from 82.3% to **74.5%**. Source: [`2026-09-21-W3-Tax-Decomposition.md`](../2026-09-21-W3-Tax-Decomposition.md).
- Event study: ordinary days, not a handful of events. Source: [`2026-09-14-P5i-Event-Study-Result.md`](../2026-09-14-P5i-Event-Study-Result.md).

## What would change the conclusion

A new print that puts the multiple, not earnings, in the lead. A sustained reversal of the premium. A revenue-recognition result large enough to move the earnings share out of the band. A rates or commodity decomposition that this file does not yet have.
