# Collateral re-use

## Hypothesis

Collateral re-use and velocity — Singh’s channel — created the money that bought equities, or levered the bid through basis trades, prime brokerage, securities lending, and margin.

## Status

**Failed as the proximate source of this equity window.** Re-use intensity did not rise. The channel stays on the map. Re-test when the next collateral census and the OFR repo print land.

## Evidence

Re-use intensity did not rise. The dated step in the six-bank collateral stock is the first half of 2026, after most of the price rise. Source: [`2026-09-15-N4-Scale-Timing-Bound.md`](../2026-09-15-N4-Scale-Timing-Bound.md) and [`2026-08-30-D10-Reuse-On-The-Measured-Chain.md`](../2026-08-30-D10-Reuse-On-The-Measured-Chain.md).

Velocity is carried as a range **about 1.3–2.0**, unsigned (C-135: the step that signed it at 1.3–1.5 did not hold; about 2.0 on Form PF securities posted alone, Singh's own figure). Source: [`2026-08-31-N3v4-Singh-Reconciliation.md`](../2026-08-31-N3v4-Singh-Reconciliation.md) (C-061). The US-five permitted-collateral panel is about **2.23×** from 2017 to mid-2026 in the monitor. That is a stock reconciliation, not a price impact.

The re-use share is flat: collateral repledged as a share of collateral the four largest dealers may repledge was **84.0%** at 30 June 2026 (82.5–85.4% on Bank of America's rounding) against **82.6%** a year earlier. By bank: JPMorgan 78.2% to 79.8%, Goldman 85.2% to 88.4%, Morgan Stanley 78.2% to 76.8%, Bank of America about 91% to 93%. The permitted stock grew about 24%, so the growth is in volume, not intensity. Source: 10-Q collateral notes listed by Grok (K3, [`_research/2026-10-13-Grok-Wave-Reviews-Return.md`](../_research/2026-10-13-Grok-Wave-Reviews-Return.md)); JPMorgan and Morgan Stanley re-read from SEC XBRL by the supervisor.

FINRA margin debit was **$1,453.8bn** in August 2026, up about **$37bn** on July. That is leverage outstanding. It is not an attribution to the index return.

No official collateral-velocity aggregate exists. The source searched FSB, BIS, and ESMA and did not find one. FINRA securities-lending dissemination is dated March 2027 on the calendar.

## What would change the conclusion

A rise in re-use intensity that precedes the price move, on a measured chain, large enough to matter beside the earnings result. A free velocity aggregate would replace the estimated range. It would not, by itself, create a causal price share.

## Data used

Dealer 10-Q collateral permitted and repledged, primary-dealer repo venue split, FICC sponsored volumes, Form PF collateral, FIA CCP initial margin (still the 2026:Q1 vintage at the 15 September pull), FINRA margin. Third-party papers in the source tree were not copied here.
