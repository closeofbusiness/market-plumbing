# Collateral re-use

## Hypothesis

Collateral re-use and velocity — Singh’s channel — created the money that bought equities, or levered the bid through basis trades, prime brokerage, securities lending, and margin.

## Status

**Failed as the proximate source of this equity window.** Re-use intensity did not rise. The channel stays on the map. Re-test when the next collateral census and the OFR repo print land.

## Evidence

Re-use intensity did not rise. The dated step in the six-bank collateral stock is the first half of 2026, after most of the price rise. Source: `2026-09-15-N4-Scale-Timing-Bound.md` and `2026-08-30-D10-Reuse-On-The-Measured-Chain.md`.

Velocity, after the point estimate was withdrawn, is carried as a range **about 1.3–1.5**, mechanism-signed, not a point. Source: `2026-08-31-N3v4-Singh-Reconciliation.md` (C-061). The US-five permitted-collateral panel is about **2.23×** from 2017 to mid-2026 in the monitor. That is a stock reconciliation, not a price impact.

FINRA margin debit was **$1,453.8bn** in August 2026, up about **$37bn** on July. That is leverage outstanding. It is not an attribution to the index return.

No official collateral-velocity aggregate exists. The source searched FSB, BIS, and ESMA and did not find one. FINRA securities-lending dissemination is dated March 2027 on the calendar.

## What would change the conclusion

A rise in re-use intensity that precedes the price move, on a measured chain, large enough to matter beside the earnings result. A free velocity aggregate would replace the estimated range. It would not, by itself, create a causal price share.

## Data used

Dealer 10-Q collateral permitted and repledged, primary-dealer repo venue split, FICC sponsored volumes, Form PF collateral, FIA CCP initial margin (still the 2026:Q1 vintage at the 15 September pull), FINRA margin. Third-party papers in the source tree were not copied here.
