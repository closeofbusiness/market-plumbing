# IPO boom

## Hypothesis

The IPO boom is a broad new-money bid into operating-company equity, and the buyers can be named from free filings.

## Status

**Sized on concentration. Open on who bought.** Hypothesis.

## Evidence

About **$112bn** of new money in 2026 to the date of the prospectus note, about **two-thirds** one deal (SpaceX, **$75bn** primary). Excluding it, about **$36.9bn**. Source: `2026-09-12-I1b-IPO-Prospectus-Facts.md`. The **$75bn** primary is also the series key `spacex_ipo_primary_bn`.

Who bought is not recoverable on free data. Cornerstones are named in 3 of 10 deals. Source: `2026-09-14-I2-IPO-Allocation.md`.

This sits under the supply result: operating companies as a sector retired equity. A concentrated IPO boom does not overturn that sector total.

## What would change the conclusion

A year in which primary issuance is broad rather than one deal, or a free allocation source that names the buyers on more than a minority of deals.

## Data used

Prospectuses and the IPO scout tables (`2026-09-11-I1-IPO-Scout.md`). No paid new-issue database.
