# Official-sector plumbing

## Hypothesis

Official balance sheets — the Federal Reserve’s reverse repo and Treasury portfolio, foreign official Treasury buying, QT and QE — were the marginal funding of the equity bid or of the Treasury curve.

## Status

**Sized for the 2024 reverse-repo handoff. Open as a rates result.** Do not read a Fed purchase or a foreign-official holding change as the cause of a yield or equity move.

## Evidence

2024 private-repo growth was the exit from the overnight reverse-repo facility, not new cash. Net of the handoff, about **−$46bn**. Source: [`2026-09-15-N4-Scale-Timing-Bound.md`](../2026-09-15-N4-Scale-Timing-Bound.md) (C-085). ON RRP in the monitor was about **$0.2bn** on 21 August 2026. Foreign official reverse repo was about **$373bn** the same week. Those are stocks.

Fed net Treasury purchases were still positive in 2026:Q1 and Q2 (**+$156.2bn**, then **+$97.8bn**). Source: series key `fed_treasury_purchases_flow_bn`, Z.1 vintage 11 September 2026. Money funds were selling Treasuries in 2026:Q2 (**−$144.9bn** on `mmf_treasury_flow_bn`). This is not a full QT/QE decomposition of the curve.

TIC Table 5, end-July 2026: foreign official US Treasuries **$3,773.1bn** (down **$5.0bn** on the month), Japan **$1,103.9bn** (down **$12.8bn**), China **$618.0bn** (down **$15.4bn**). Source: [`_research/2026-09-17-TIC-July-2026.md`](../_research/2026-09-17-TIC-July-2026.md) and [`2026-09-11-Who-Buys-Treasuries-Synthesis.md`](../2026-09-11-Who-Buys-Treasuries-Synthesis.md). A holding change is the measurement. It is not a curve attribution.

## What would change the conclusion

A rates decomposition (carry, roll, expected path, term premium) that leaves a residual, and a demand table on the same denominator that can be compared with that residual without calling the residual a cause. That decomposition is not in the source.

## Data used

FRED RRP and reserves, SOMA, Z.1 Fed and money-fund Treasury flows, TIC Table 5 and CSLT. Next prints are on the calendar extract in the monitor.
