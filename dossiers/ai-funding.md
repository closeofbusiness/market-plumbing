# AI funding

## Hypothesis

The AI build-out was funded by new money creation, shadow credit, or a broad bond bid. The alternative is that it was funded from operating cash, with the real innovation off balance sheet.

## Status

**Sized.** Mostly self-funded. Hypothesis, re-test on the next 10-Qs and on Oracle’s guarantee maturity. This is a stress case of the equity question, not a separate project.

## Evidence

Three of the five large AI spenders (Alphabet, Meta, Microsoft) generate more operating cash than their capex; NVIDIA, the sixth firm in the funding table, spends little (C-134). Amazon does not (operating cash $161.4bn against capex $173.0bn, twelve months to 30 Jun 2026), and nor does Oracle (operating cash $46.9bn against capex $75.7bn; the internal-cash gap in the monitor is about **−$35bn** at 31 August 2026). Sources: [`2026-09-12-F1-Funding-Table.md`](../2026-09-12-F1-Funding-Table.md), [`2026-09-12-Oracle-RPO-And-Financing.md`](../2026-09-12-Oracle-RPO-And-Financing.md).

Lease commitments signed but not yet commenced sum to **$1,122.9bn**, about **4.9×** the **$228.3bn** of guarantees contracted. The “13×” figure divided future leases by live guarantees only and was corrected (C-116). Live guarantees were **$86.2bn** at the 22 August reading, which has a known decay date. Source: [`2026-08-22-Guarantee-Stack.md`](../2026-08-22-Guarantee-Stack.md).

Oracle remaining performance obligations were **$664bn** at 31 August 2026, against **$455bn** a year earlier, with **$288bn** of additional data-centre lease commitments off balance sheet. Source: the 15 September calendar row and [`2026-09-12-Oracle-RPO-And-Financing.md`](../2026-09-12-Oracle-RPO-And-Financing.md).

**2026 bonds, year to date (13 Oct): about $229bn** of the hyperscalers' own bonds:
- Alphabet $81bn, Amazon $98bn, Meta $25bn, Oracle $25bn, Microsoft none.
- All currencies, at ECB rates on each pricing date; deals from the 8-Ks, listed by Grok.
- The sum matches Goldman's $194bn to 3 August and JPMorgan's $219bn to 20 August.

The "$750bn" in the press is the 2026 capex forecast, not borrowing. Source: [`_research/2026-10-13-Grok-Wave-Reviews-Return.md`](../_research/2026-10-13-Grok-Wave-Reviews-Return.md) (R1).

The debt that does exist sits in a corporate bond market held mainly by long money; the AI names are not a separate Z.1 line, and free data count only two holder groups for them (C-139). The hyperscalers were 3% of outstanding US investment-grade debt at end-2025 but over 15% of 2026 issuance by early May (Bank of England, July 2026 FSR), and are close to 5% of the Bloomberg US IG corporate index, 7.5% at 10 years and over (CPRAM, Sep 2026). See the long-money dossier. Amazon’s **$17.5bn** delayed-draw term loan was undrawn at 30 June 2026; the draw window is a calendar item, not a completed taking of bank money.

## What would change the conclusion

Hyperscaler free cash flow crossing zero and staying there. A draw of the delayed-draw loan, or a roll of the maturing guarantees that enlarges the live stack. A private-credit or SPV leg that the filings do not yet show, large beside the lease stack.

## Data used

SEC filings (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K) for six firms, hand-read lease notes, XBRL cash and debt where tagged. There is no standing pull script for the lease stack. Next hand-read is on the calendar for mid-November 2026.
