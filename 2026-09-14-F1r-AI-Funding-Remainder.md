# F1r — the AI build-out's four unsourced legs, closed (14 September 2026)

## 1. Amazon's $17.5bn term loan — UNDRAWN, and filings will not tell us in time

**No draw, cancellation or amendment has been disclosed as of 14 September 2026**, sixteen days before the
window closes. **Verified independently by me:** Amazon filed 47 documents between 8 June and 11 September;
five are 8-Ks; **exactly one carries Item 1.01 or 2.03 — the original 10 June filing that created the
facility** (acc. 0001104659-26-072140). A draw would require a new one. There is none.

The facility's own words: *"We may draw up to $17.5 billion in a single draw on any business day on or prior
to September 30, 2026, after which any undrawn commitments will automatically terminate."* The 10-Q for the
quarter ended 30 June states verbatim: *"There were no borrowings outstanding under the Term Loan as of
June 30, 2026."*

**The timing trap, and it matters for the calendar row:** Amazon's Q3 10-Q — the first statement that would
show a post-window balance — **is not due until late October or November, after the 30 September deadline.**
So the calendar row cannot be closed on the 30th by reading a filing. Either an 8-K appears (a draw is a
material definitive agreement) or we wait six weeks to learn it expired unused.

## 2. NVIDIA's $108.5bn against $56bn — NOT an inconsistency, and now resolved

Three separate populations, all in Note 10 of the 10-Q for the quarter ended 26 July 2026, all as of the
same date. **I fetched the filing and confirmed every heading and figure verbatim.**

| table | what it is | total |
|---|---|---:|
| "Additional Commitments" | **firm** commitments: AI-cloud agreements $36bn + data-centre leases not yet commenced $20bn | **$56bn** |
| "maximum gross exposure related to our guarantees" | **contingent** exposure if a customer defaults | **$108.5bn** |
| "Future commitments by fiscal year" | NVIDIA's own supply, manufacturing and R&D commitments | **$366bn** |

**They were never meant to reconcile.** A firm commitment and a maximum contingent exposure are different
objects; our note flagging them as inconsistent was the error. Closed.

**And the guarantee is worth restating with the detail we now have, verbatim from the filing:**

> *"In August 2026, we entered into guarantees, capped at a total of $105 billion, to provide credit support
> on a land, power, and shell buildout with affiliates of SB Energy Corp. (SB Energy) on behalf of a
> customer, an affiliate of OpenAI Group PBC (OpenAI), related to leases for approximately 4.25 gigawatts
> of IT load."*

We already carried "$105bn of an OpenAI affiliate's leases." We now have the counterparty (SB Energy), the
date (August 2026) and the scale (**4.25 GW**).

named entity from public filings.** That is the same shape as the household residual, one layer down.

## 4. Private credit and data centres — the series cannot exist, and here is why

**Confirmed structurally, not just "not found".** The SEC's own Private Fund Statistics (2025Q4, latest)
classifies all **58,891** reported private funds by *legal type only* — Private Equity, Hedge, Real Estate,
Venture Capital, Securitized Asset, Other, Liquidity. **Zero mentions of "data center" or "digital
infrastructure" in the 69-page report.** The taxonomy is structurally incapable of a sector breakout, so no
amount of searching will produce one. That closes the question properly rather than leaving it open.

**But a first data point does exist, at the BDC level**, because BDCs file public schedules of investments.
Ares Capital's FY2025 10-K industry-classifies a holding as **"Data Centers"** (XBRL tag
`arcc:DataCentersMember`): a first-lien senior secured loan to **Retained Vantage Data Centers Intermediate
Holdco, LP**, 9.50%, maturing 12/2031, cost $40.8mn / fair value $40.1mn at 31 Dec 2025, **plus a separate
entirely undrawn $155.1mn revolving and delayed-draw commitment to the same borrower.**

Blackstone's BCRED carries an explicit data-centre risk factor but no named holding surfaced; Blue Owl's OTF
describes data-centre lending only as a forward possibility. **This was three BDCs read in full out of 27
candidates — a first data point, not a census.**
