# Corrections and closed designs

The upstream register is `CORRECTIONS.md` (248,516 bytes at 23 September 2026, correction numbers through at least C-119 in the live answer). This file does not copy that log. It records the closed designs and the bans that follow from them, in the programme’s own words.

A correction is how the programme keeps negatives. A claim that survived last month is not settled.

## Causal impact on equities — closed

**Object.** How much a dollar of net buying, by investors who are not responding to price, moves the aggregate value of US equities. Call it M. Description of who bought what does not need M. An attribution that multiplies a flow by M does.

**Finding.** Free data cannot deliver that aggregate causal estimate. Every free route that was tried is closed. Source: `2026-09-16-TierB-E005-Resolution-Limit.md`, frame `2026-09-13-B0-The-Bridge-Frame.md`.

| Design | Why it closed | Not what it means |
|---|---|---|
| B5, sovereign-mandate instrument | Data. Mandate bands never bound; monthly triggers unobservable. First stage weak (n=9). | Not “no economic effect”. |
| P4, Russell reconstitution | Data. Free substitutes never assigned treatment (C-086). First-stage *F* about 2.6. | Not a market-level elasticity. |
| B6, dividend payment-day | Arithmetic (C-090, C-091). About 2.5bp of predicted gap inside about 111bp of daily noise at a borrowed multiplier near 1.9, which is on the order of centuries of data. At the top of the published envelope the same algebra needs about 16 years against 11.7 years on hand. | Not “the mechanism failed”. Not “blocked on missing pay dates”. |
| JVZ, mega-firm passive test | Underpower (C-092). Corrected placebo minimum detectable effect on returns is about 1.10 percentage points against a predicted 0.53, with 26 free N-PORT quarters. Parity on elapsed time alone is about 113 quarters. | Not “not supported”. Not a rejection. |

**Bans that follow**

- No scalar M, and no “flows explain X% of 2024–26 via multiplier Y”.
- No 2024–26 causal attribution from these designs.
- Do not reopen B6 in order to fetch pay dates.
- Do not quote the underpowered flow correlations as findings.
- Do not say the mega-firm test refuted passive amplification.

The charter’s further ban — do not claim that FX, official buying, or financial commodity flows caused a yield or curve move — is the same distinction (measurement versus cause) applied where a design has not even been run. It is carried so those markets do not inherit a causal sentence from the equity work.

## Other closures that the live answer depends on

These are hypotheses that were withdrawn or narrowed. The owner file is the authority if a number here and a number there ever differ.

- **C-077.** Do not borrow a multiplier from the literature and apply it to this window.
- **C-078.** The window compression of the equity premium stands. A constant-premium counterfactual level does not.
- **C-080.** Net issuance is the wrong denominator for a demand-shift multiplier.
- **C-081, C-083.** Z.1 corporate-equity issuance counts ETF shares. The operating-company figure is the nonfinancial corporate line.
- **C-084.** The household line is still unsplit by legal entity. Private-fund statistics do not bound private equity inside it.
- **C-085.** 2024 wholesale-repo growth was the reverse-repo handoff.
- **C-089.** Money creation is not a funding ceiling, and it is not “too small to have mattered” as a slogan.
- **C-098.** A residual solved from an identity is the weakest number in the table. The earnings decomposition’s third term was withdrawn on that ground.
- **C-102.** “Premium compression” is mostly the mirror of the real-yield rise.
- **C-107, C-108.** “Net of the wrapper” is not the operating-company number. A decade of repurchases versus gross issuance mixed public and private issuance.
- **C-111.** ETF versus mutual-fund offset is consistent with rotation and is not a measurement of it.
- **C-116.** Lease stack versus guarantees is about 4.9× on a like-for-like future-versus-future comparison, not 13×.
- **C-118.** Shadow money is not ruled out as a ceiling over the full window. It is ruled out as the 2024 source of new cash.

## What is not closed

Rates, FX, and commodities have no parallel causal design to close, because the price decomposition has not been run. Leaving them open is not a finding that official buying or financial commodity demand did nothing. It is a refusal to write the cause before the measurement.
