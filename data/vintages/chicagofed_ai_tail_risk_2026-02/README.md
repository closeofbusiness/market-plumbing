# Chicago Fed Insights, February 2026 — "Tail Risk for Banks Posed by Investments in Generative Artificial Intelligence"

Cohen, Killen, Lau (Financial Economics). Fetched 23 Sep 2026, HTTP 200, generic user agent.
URL: https://www.chicagofed.org/publications/chicago-fed-insights/2026/ai-tail-risk-for-banks

Saved because D2 (22 Aug) cited this article as "fetched and read" with no URL and no copy, and W2 then
rested on it. See C-115. The facts it carries, as stated in the article (not re-derived by us):

- Large-bank C&I exposure to AI-adjacent industries: ~$450bn committed, ~$150bn outstanding, late 2025.
- "AI-adjacent" = C&I and CRE loans to AI software and AI infrastructure companies, data-centre
  construction, and loans secured by data centres. The $450bn/$150bn figure is the C&I exposure.
- The same concentration was ~9% of total commitments (~$250bn) in 2015, rising to 13% (~$450bn).
- JPMorgan analysts' estimate of AI-company-issued debt ~$1.2trn, presented in contrast to bank debt
  ("over double" the commitments, "eight times" the outstanding balance). An analyst estimate, relayed.
- Outstanding AI-adjacent exposure ~0.8% of bank total assets and ~9% of tier 1 capital on average;
  delinquencies in line with the overall portfolio. Software C&I commitments $150bn (early 2022) to $191bn (late 2025).
