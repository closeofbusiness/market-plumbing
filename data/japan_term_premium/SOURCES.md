Each source below is public and free. Request times are in `fetch_log.csv`. Raw downloads stay in a cache outside the repository. This is one model's split of the yield change. Other models split it differently, and it does not show why yields rose.

## Column provenance

- `decomposition.csv`, `verdict.csv`, `vintage_revisions.csv`: changes and shares computed here from the files above; no level is committed.
- `fitted_check.csv`: gaps between the file's term premium plus expected rate and the Ministry of Finance yield.
- `pins.csv`: single published values, at most four per Nakajima vintage, three for JMA, four for the Ministry of Finance.
- `vintages.csv`: commits, blob identities, sha256 of the downloaded bytes and the logged retrieval times.

## Build decisions

These are choices the builder made where the specification was silent or inconsistent, and the manual paper reading. They matter to a reviewer, not to a reader of the result.

1. A bridge window (July to August 2024) whose yield change is under 0.25 percentage points reads "not classified", because the specification's checks are applied in the order written; otherwise it reads "not applicable". Both leave the share and the distance blank, as the rule says for any unclassified window.
2. The band test uses the share rounded to 9 decimals, and the 0.25 test uses the yield change rounded to 9 decimals, so floating-point error cannot move an edge. The published cells carry 4 decimals, so no real value is moved.
3. The daily file is compared with the Ministry of Finance yield on each common date at a tolerance of 0.00015 percentage points, not 1e-6. Section 3.7 of the specification and its selftest table give 1e-6 (the supervisor's round-2 ruling on the daily-file tolerance). The round-4 ruling limits 1e-6 to values the build recomputes from cells it already holds, and the later ruling wins. The 1e-6 test is applied to the monthly means of the daily rows, recomputed a second way.
4. Leak rules read strictly. The first-period column of `vintages.csv` gives the year only, and the last-period column gives a month only when it is one of the seven listed months (otherwise blank). No daily date is committed anywhere. The two level checks (the pinned-level check and the revisions check) say match or mismatch and nothing else. The date at which the Ministry of Finance file ends is described in words, not as a date.
5. The headline reading, the JMA label, the list of JMA arms that fired and the Bank-matched share are filled on the 10-year verdict row only, because each is a statement about the 10-year result. The other tenors carry their own share, distance and classification, the daily-file share and the JMA share.
6. For the JMA ratio arm, when the monthly file's change in term premium is not positive, the arm compares magnitudes and fires on a sign flip. For the positive case it is the literal rule: under half or over twice.
7. The cache must resolve outside the repository (the specification's rule that raw files stay outside it). A cache inside the repository is refused even if git ignores it, which is stricter than the hedged-yield build.
8. On a hard check failure the build writes every output into a `_FAILED/` folder inside the cache and leaves the committed folder alone, so a failed build cannot register a verdict.
9. Paper reading (a manual step, from the supervisor's round-3 and round-4 rulings on the model caveat), 19 October 2026: one request for each PDF, with the neutral identification string, HTTP 200, no redirect, no retry, on hosts outside the script's allowlist. Ichiue and Ueno (2013): https://www.boj.or.jp/en/research/wps_rev/wps_2013/data/wp13e08.pdf (444,767 bytes). Nakajima (2025): https://www.ier.hit-u.ac.jp/Common/publication/DP/DPS-A760.pdf (545,272 bytes). The links come from the Sites page already in the cache (the Bank's link there is to the paper's landing page; the PDF address follows the Bank's pattern for its other papers). The specification's "pages 8 to 9" are PDF pages; the statement that rho is the long-run level of the short rate is on printed page 7 (PDF page 8). The equations were read from page images, because the text extraction drops the symbols.
10. The JMA rows use the same rule as the monthly file, with the yield change taken as the sum of the term-premium change and the expected-rate change. The JMA shape check tests that expected rate plus term premium equals the yield within 0.0002 on every actual row.
11. Two text fixes after the first build, made because the first wording was wrong, not because of a rule. (a) The README's revision paragraph first read: "The one revision seen is small next to those margins; a change of model is not." That was written from the specification's single observed revision; the cache holds four, so the paragraph is now computed from the revision rows (the largest move in the 10-year share between vintages on the revision windows, set against the registered share's distance to each edge of the band). (b) The revisions check on the first window month first printed only "differs"; it now says that the term-premium and expected-rate columns of that month are identical in all three vintages and that another column differs in the older vintage (the specification had observed July against October only). No frozen value, rule or threshold changed.
12. The leak guard's consecutive-level test looks at columns with 3 or more decimals (2 decimals would match by chance). Its level-in-checks test is structural (named levels, numbers in level checks, pinned values), because a plain value match collides with legitimate changes.
