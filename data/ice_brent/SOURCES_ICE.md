# Sources and terms for the ICE Brent positioning ledger (data/ice_brent/)

Derived from ICE Futures Europe, Commitments of Traders historical files (ice.com/publicdocs/futures). Statistics only; no ICE record is reproduced. Non-commercial research (THE_ASK.md E-025).

- **ICE.** The inputs are the yearly `COTHist<YYYY>.csv` files that ICE Futures Europe publishes at `https://www.ice.com/publicdocs/futures/`, 2011 to the report year (16 files at the 15 October 2026 build). ICE's website terms forbid automated retrieval. The principal ruled that the Brent files may be scripted anyway, on the conditions of the DTCC swap-tape ruling (THE_ASK.md E-025, extending E-024): non-commercial use; no raw ICE file or row is committed or redistributed; only derived statistics are published and they cite ICE; requests are sequential and paced; and any technical refusal ends the run instead of being routed around. The ruling covers the Brent COT files only, not other ICE data such as prices or settlement files. The raw files are kept outside the repository (`~/ice_cot/`), are read in memory and are never committed. Requests carry the project's neutral User-Agent with no name or address. `fetch_log.csv` records every request.
- **EIA.** The Europe Brent spot price (FOB, dollars per barrel) from the U.S. Energy Information Administration, `https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/hist_xls/RBRTEd.xls`. U.S. Government work, public domain. It is committed as `raw/eia_RBRTEd.xls` with a row in `data/THIRD_PARTY_MANIFEST.tsv`. It is a spot assessment, not the ICE Brent futures settlement (`usd_basis` says so on every ledger row).
- **Licence.** CC BY 4.0 (the repository's LICENSE) covers only this project's computation and write-up. It does not extend to ICE or to any ICE data; the use of ICE's data is non-commercial under THE_ASK.md E-025, and nothing in this folder licenses anyone else to use ICE's data commercially. A rights-holder who wants something removed can ask through a repository issue (DISCLAIMER.md).
- **Retrieval dates.** Every CSV starts with a `# source:` line naming ICE and the retrieval dates. Readers must skip lines that start with `#`. The dates come from `fetch_log.csv`, so an offline rebuild reproduces the files byte for byte. Files that were already in the cache when the script first ran are logged as `adopted_from_cache` and carry their file modification time as the retrieval time.
- **What the published percentages let a reader back-calculate.** The ledger publishes managed-money net (in contracts) and its share of open interest to 0.1 percentage point, for both report types. Dividing the first by the second gives an estimate of open interest, which ICE's files carry as a gross figure and which this repository does not publish. The estimate is good to the rounding of the percentage: a half-width of 0.05 percentage point over the share. Measured at the 15 October 2026 build over the 823 weeks and two report types (the 1,645 week-types where the share does not round to 0.0), that half-width has a median of 0.57% and a 90th percentile of 1.06%; the realised error against ICE's figure has a median of 0.27%, a 90th percentile of 0.65% and a maximum of 12.0% (in a week when the share was small). `checks.csv` K17 records these. So open interest is recoverable to about half a percent in a typical week. Gross long and short positions, in total or by class, **cannot** be recovered from what is published: the ledger carries only nets, and a net fixes the difference between a long and a short, not either of them.
- **Nothing here says a position moves a price.** The ledger measures who holds the two sides. See README.md for the columns and the caveats.
