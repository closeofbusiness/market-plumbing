# Sources and column provenance

Generated by `python3 bin/fx_rate_gap.py build`. Every web fact was fetched by `fetch` with the project's neutral identification string; `fetch_log.csv` lists each request with its time, status, size and sha256. Retrieval dates below are read from that log.

## U.S. Department of the Treasury (home.treasury.gov)

- URL: https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/daily-treasury-rates.csv/{Y}/all?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value={Y}&page&_format=csv, Y = 2017 to 2026 (10 files)
- Retrieved: 2026-10-18
- Licence: A US government work; the catalogue's CC0 notice was not read.
- Citation: Source: U.S. Department of the Treasury, Daily Treasury Par Yield Curve Rates.

## Ministry of Finance, Japan (www.mof.go.jp)

- URL: https://www.mof.go.jp/english/policy/jgbs/reference/interest_rate/historical/jgbcme_all.csv; https://www.mof.go.jp/english/policy/international_policy/reference/feio/foreign_exchange_intervention_operations.csv; the monthly pages under https://www.mof.go.jp/english/policy/international_policy/reference/feio/monthly/ (20260731e, 20260828e, 20260930e)
- Retrieved: 2026-10-18
- Licence: Public Data License 1.0, with numerical data outside copyright (as relayed in the specification). The Japan Securities Dealers Association's rights behind the yields were not read.
- Citation: Source: Ministry of Finance Japan, Interest Rate (JGB constant maturity) and Foreign Exchange Intervention Operations.

## European Central Bank (data-api.ecb.europa.eu)

- URL: https://data-api.ecb.europa.eu/service/data/YC/B.U2.EUR.4F.G_N_A.SV_C_YM.BETA0+BETA1+BETA2+BETA3+TAU1+TAU2+SR_2Y+SR_10Y+PY_2Y+PY_10Y?format=csvdata&startPeriod=2017-12-01; https://data-api.ecb.europa.eu/service/data/EXR/D.JPY+USD.EUR.SP00.A?format=csvdata&startPeriod=2017-12-01
- Retrieved: 2026-10-18
- Licence: Reuse with the source named, unmodified (as relayed in the specification). The semiannual par yield is a project computation from the ECB's parameters, not an ECB statistic. Data note: the euro gap uses the zero curve built from those parameters (it reproduces the published zero yields, SR, to 1e-10 on every date but one), not the ECB's published par yields (PY), which do not follow the parameters before 2024; `checks.csv` C05 prints WARN for this and the supervisor accepted it on that ground.
- Citation: Source: ECB statistics, euro area yield curves (AAA, Svensson) and euro foreign exchange reference rates.

## Bank for International Settlements (stats.bis.org)

- URL: https://stats.bis.org/api/v2/data/dataflow/BIS/WS_XRU/1.0/D.JP+XM.JPY+EUR.A?startPeriod=2017-12-01&format=csv
- Retrieved: 2026-10-18
- Licence: Reuse with citation (data.bis.org/help/legal, as relayed in the specification).
- Citation: Source: Bank for International Settlements, daily exchange rates (WS_XRU), series D.JP.JPY.A and D.XM.EUR.A.

## Deutsche Bundesbank (api.statistiken.bundesbank.de)

- URL: https://api.statistiken.bundesbank.de/rest/data/BBSIS/D.I.ZST.ZI.EUR.S1311.B.A604.R02XX+R10XX.R.A.A._Z._Z.A?format=csv&lang=en&startPeriod=2017-12-01
- Retrieved: 2026-10-18
- Licence: Terms page not read in this pass. Used only for one correlation and a list of dates in `checks.csv`; no Bundesbank value is committed.
- Citation: Source: Deutsche Bundesbank, time series BBSIS.D.I.ZST.ZI.EUR.S1311.B.A604.R02XX/R10XX.

## Which weekly-panel columns are source values and which are computed

The weekly panel itself (`data/vintages/fx_rate_gap/weekly_panel.csv`) is not committed.

| Column | Kind | Origin |
|---|---|---|
| `usdjpy`, `eur_per_usd` | source value | BIS WS_XRU daily rates on `fx_date` (checked against the ECB reference cross) |
| `us2`, `us10`, `us2_sd`, `us10_sd` | source value | Treasury par yields, 2 Yr and 10 Yr |
| `jp2`, `jp10` | source value | MoF constant-maturity yields, 2Y and 10Y |
| `ea2_sa`, `ea10_sa` | computed | semiannual par yield from the ECB AAA Svensson parameters of `ea_date` |
| `week_end`, `fx_date`, `us_date`, `us_date_sd`, `jp_date`, `ea_date` | computed | the alignment rule of specification 3.1 |
| `gap_*`, `slope_gap_*` | computed | US minus foreign, in percentage points; slope gap = 10-year gap minus 2-year gap |
| `dln_jpy`, `dln_eur`, `d_*` | computed | weekly log change and weekly change in the level |
| `drop_reason` | computed | intervention weeks from the MoF files; stale legs from the 4-10 day span rule |
