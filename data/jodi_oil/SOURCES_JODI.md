# Sources and terms for the JODI world oil ledger (data/jodi_oil/)

Derived from the JODI-Oil World Database of the International Energy Forum (IEF). Statistics only; no JODI file, row or country-by-month table is reproduced here.

- **JODI.** The inputs are the annual primary CSV files on JODI's data-downloads page (`https://www.jodidata.org/oil/database/data-downloads.aspx`, the links under `annual-csv/primary/`), 2015 to 2026 (12 files at the 18 October 2026 build; the 2026 file is named `primaryyear2026.csv`). The citation is the first line above, in the form JODI's user guide gives under "How to Cite JODI Data". Any note or page that quotes a number from this folder must carry it.
- **Rights.** JODI's terms of use (`terms-of-use.aspx`, section Intellectual Property) say "All such rights are reserved". The terms contain no clause on scripted access (read on 18 October 2026), and the script fetches under this project's house rules: one request at a time, at least 5.05 seconds apart, the neutral User-Agent with no name or address, no redirect followed, and a stop file (`~/jodi_oil/STOP`) written on any refusal. No JODI file is redistributed.
- **What is cached and what is committed.** The JODI CSVs are cached only in `~/jodi_oil/` (outside the repository) as `jodi_primary_<YYYY>_<sha12>.csv`, so every vintage is kept. The script refuses a cache path inside the repository; `.gitignore` lists `jodi_primary_*.csv`, `primaryyear*.csv` and `secondaryyear*.csv`; and `bin/check.sh` M12 looks for the rest. What is committed is derived: sums over fixed panels, coverage ratios and counts for the changing set of reporters, a fixed list of at most 30 checkpoint rows, and checks.
- **Disclosure rule.** A barrel sum is published only for a fixed panel of at least three members whose membership is constant over the window, so no entry or exit can be differenced out of it. If two published panels of the same series differ by one or two countries the larger is blanked. The changing reporter groups appear only as coverage ratios rounded to 0.01 (one point is about 0.79 mb/d of STEO world crude, which blurs any single entrant below that size) and as counts, never as barrel sums or barrel changes. Country-level values appear only in `checkpoints.csv`. Reporter sums are printed to the terminal and never written to a file.
- **Licence.** CC BY 4.0 (the repository's LICENSE) covers only this project's computation and write-up. It does not extend to JODI or to any JODI data, and nothing in this folder licenses anyone to use JODI's data.
- **IEA origin of OECD history.** For OECD members JODI's history (every month but the latest) is IEA Monthly Oil Statistics, and the US latest month comes from EIA's weekly reports (JODI's country notes). OECD figures here are therefore not independent of EIA's own STEO inventories, which also rest on IEA data. IEA's own licence for that content was not re-read; only derived aggregates are committed (OPEN-6 in the specification).
- **EIA.** The Petroleum Supply Monthly series `MCRSTUS1` (US ending stocks of crude oil, including the SPR), `MCESTUS1` (excluding the SPR), `MCSSTUS1` (the SPR) and `MCRFPUS2` (US field production of crude oil) come from `https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/hist_xls/<ID>m.xls`; the Short-Term Energy Outlook series from the bulk file `https://www.eia.gov/opendata/bulk/STEO.zip`. The release date in the acknowledgement line (6 October 2026) is the `last_updated` of the STEO entry in EIA's bulk manifest, `https://www.eia.gov/opendata/bulk/manifest.txt`, fetched on 18 October 2026 (`raw/eia_steo_release.txt`). EIA is a U.S. Government agency; its work is in the public domain (17 U.S.C. 105). The four workbooks and an extract of the STEO series used (`raw/eia_steo_extract.csv`) are committed, with rows in `data/THIRD_PARTY_MANIFEST.tsv`, so the ledger rebuilds offline except for the JODI files.
- **Retrieval dates.** Every CSV starts with a `# source:` line naming JODI and the retrieval date. Readers must skip lines that start with `#`. The date comes from `fetch_log.csv` (the newest JODI file in it), so an offline rebuild reproduces the files byte for byte.
- **Nothing here is a world figure.** See README.md for what the ledger can and cannot say.
