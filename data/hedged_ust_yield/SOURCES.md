# Sources and terms for data/hedged_ust_yield/

Each source below is public and free. Retrieval dates are in `fetch_log.csv`. The committed files are derived statistics; raw downloads, the daily panel and the yen monthly rows are in a gitignored cache. This measures an incentive. It does not show what led any investor to buy, sell or hedge.

## Column provenance

- `ust10_pct`: U.S. Treasury 10 Yr par yield, monthly mean of daily values (US government work).
- `home10_pct`: euro rows: ECB AAA Svensson parameters, 10-year par yield recomputed on a semiannual basis, monthly mean (derived from ECB statistics).
- `rate_gap_term_pp, basis_term_pp, hedged_carry_pct, pickup_pp, form_gap_bp`: computed here from the monthly OIS and cross-currency medians of the swap-tape ledger (DTCC), the Treasury and ECB yields, and, for the diagnostics, SOFR, EFFR and the euro short-term rate.
- `n_ois_us, n_ois_home, n_basis`: counts from the ledger's monthly cells (DTCC).
- `yen_summary.csv value`: annual means of the above for the yen pair, with TONA (Bank of Japan) among the inputs; the credit is in each row.
- `references.csv published`: the figures named in each row's source; build_value is this folder's computation.
- `pins.csv`: single published values, at most five per source.
